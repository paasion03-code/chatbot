About Us

Dr. Joseph Prasad holds respected certifications and deep spiritual lineage in meditation and tantra traditions. Alongside his band of calibrated trainers worldwide, he ensures the highest quality of teaching rooted in authoritative lineages and personal experience.

Certifications Include:

  • Certified Meditation Trainer, Osho International Commune, Pune, India - focused on Dynamic and Cathartic meditation for the modern mind.
  • Tantra Practitioners' Program under Swami Mohan, Jaffna, Sri Lanka - mastering energy play, deep-rooted connections, and sacred life force integration.
  • Eastern Mysticism Course by Swami Rajendra Sharma, Uttarakhand - mastery over ancient Indian spiritual traditions and Himalayan esoteric wisdom.
