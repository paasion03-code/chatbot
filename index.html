Our Philosophy

At the Indian School of Meditation, we believe in nurturing the mind and soul through the ancient practise of meditation. Our approach blends traditional techniques with modern insights to create a holistic experience.

Calibration

Ensuring every practice and program is delivered by trainers whose own consciousness has been rigorously refined and certified by leading global and traditional schools.

Holism

Offering a diverse spectrum of practices—from Active meditations to silent Vipassana retreats and Tantra—to cater to the unique energy and needs of every seeker.

Integration

Providing an all-encompassing experience that nourishes the body, mind, and soul, recognizing that true spiritual growth requires a foundation of physical and emotional well-being.

Our Esteemed Host and Lineage

The ISM experience is anchored by the wisdom and personalized guidance of our host, Dr. Joseph Prasad. Joseph brings a wealth of authentic, multi-disciplinary focused experience, ensuring a program that is both grounded and expansive.
