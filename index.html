Our Philosophy
Calibration

All programs delivered by trainers calibrated and certified at global and traditional schools, ensuring a refined teaching experience.

Holism

Wide spectrum of practices: Vipassana, Active Meditation, Tantra retreats—catering to the unique energy and needs of every seeker.

Integration

All-encompassing approach that nourishes the body, mind, and soul—true spiritual growth includes physical and emotional well-being.

Programs & About
About Us & Host

Dr. Joseph Prasad, certified meditation trainer (Osho, Pune), tantra practitioner (Swami Mohan, Srilanka), and Eastern mysticism expert (Swami Rajendra Sharma, Uttarakhand), leads a band of global trainers for ISM’s holistic offerings.

Comprehensive Programs

Daily sessions, weekly and monthly courses, group & one-to-one practices (offline & online) and flagship Train-the-Trainer Certification (T3P).

Suite Living
